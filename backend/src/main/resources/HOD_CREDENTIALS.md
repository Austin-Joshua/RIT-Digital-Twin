### First year and H&S (SANDH)

- **There are no separate “H&S students”.** Every student belongs to a branch (CSE, ECE, MECH, etc.) from year 1 in the system. H&S is **only a reporting view** for first year.
- **First year:** All first-year students (from every branch) are *considered* under **H&S** for reporting. Their records and reports go to the **H&S HOD** (hod_sandh@ritchennai.edu.in). The CSE/ECE/etc. HODs do **not** see first-year students.
- **From second year:** The same students (still in their branch) are shown to their **branch department HOD**. So when they enter second year they “go into” their respective department for reporting — they were always in that department in the database.
